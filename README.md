Business Performance Insights

The dashboard provides an overview of sales, profitability, products, segments, and country-level performance.

The business generated approximately $118.73M in total sales and $16.89M in profit, resulting in an overall profit margin of approximately 14.23%. The Government segment generated the highest sales at approximately $52.50M, while Paseo was the top-performing product with approximately $33.01M in sales.

At the country level, the United States generated the highest sales at approximately $25.03M, followed closely by Canada. The business also experienced substantially higher sales in 2014, with approximately $92.31M compared with $26.42M in 2013.

One important area highlighted by the dashboard is the Enterprise segment. Although it generated approximately $19.61M in sales, it recorded a loss of around $0.61M. This indicates that stakeholders should review the costs, discounts, and pricing associated with this segment.

Overall, the dashboard helps stakeholders identify strong-performing products and segments while highlighting areas where profitability can be improved.
